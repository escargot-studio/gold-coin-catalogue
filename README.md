# Gold Coin Catalogue

A photo catalogue of gold bullion coins from the UK, Australia and the US, with both sides of every design. The UK view covers British gold that is sterling legal tender and so exempt from UK Capital Gains Tax.

**Live page:** https://escargot-studio.github.io/gold-coin-catalogue/ (the page itself is `gold-coin-catalogue.html`; `index.html` just forwards to it)

- Filter by series, gold content (1 oz, 1/2, 1/4, 1/10 oz and more, or a grams range), stock, years and monarch.
- Hover a coin to flip it from reverse to obverse; the Reverse / Obverse switch flips them all.
- Click a coin for both sides, specs per size, CGT status and stock.

Photos are linked from The Royal Mint, dealers (Chards, AGAU News, Tax Free Gold and others) and museum open-access collections; credits are on each coin's record. Stock and prices are a dated snapshot. CGT notes are a reference, not tax advice.
